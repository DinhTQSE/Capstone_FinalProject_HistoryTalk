\texttt{User}\newline(\texttt{AccountStatus}) & \texttt{ACTIVE}, \texttt{DEACTIVATED} & \texttt{ACTIVE} $\leftrightarrow$ \texttt{DEACTIVATED} & Admin deactivates/restores user (\texttt{AdminUserController}); End-of-year Grade 12 rollover (\texttt{AcademicLifecycleServiceImpl}). \\
\hline
\texttt{School}\newline(\texttt{SchoolStatus}) & \texttt{ACTIVE}, \texttt{INACTIVE}, \texttt{SUSPENDED}, \texttt{EXPIRED} & \texttt{ACTIVE} $\leftrightarrow$ \texttt{SUSPENDED}\newline\texttt{ACTIVE} $\leftrightarrow$ \texttt{EXPIRED}\newline\texttt{ACTIVE}/\texttt{EXPIRED} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{INACTIVE} & System Admin updates status or renews package (\texttt{AdminSchoolController}); Subscription expiry check after \texttt{31/05}. \\

\texttt{Assignment}\newline(\texttt{AssignmentStatus}) & \texttt{ACTIVE}, \texttt{CANCELLED}, \texttt{ARCHIVED} & \texttt{ACTIVE} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{ARCHIVED}\newline\texttt{ACTIVE} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{CANCELLED} & Teacher creates, cancels, or archives classroom assignment (\texttt{TeacherAssignmentController}). \\
